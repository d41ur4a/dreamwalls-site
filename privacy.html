Privacy notice

Last updated 23 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to information you send through this website. It is written to match how the site actually works today: there is no account, no database and no analytics on this site.

What the enquiry form does

The form asks for your name, phone number, and optionally your email, city, pincode, approximate area, what you are looking for and a message. When you press “Send on WhatsApp”, the site composes that information into a WhatsApp message and opens WhatsApp on your device. You then choose whether to send it.

Nothing you type is transmitted to or stored on this website. There is no server-side form handler — the message only reaches Dreamwalls if you send it from your own WhatsApp, or choose the “Email instead” link, which opens your own mail app.

What we do with an enquiry

If you send an enquiry, Dreamwalls Paints uses your details only to respond to it — a specification, a quantity estimate, or the details of the nearest depot or dealer. We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to a marketing list.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies of its own and does not run an analytics or advertising tool. If analytics is added later, this notice will say so before it goes live.

Third-party services

  • WhatsApp — if you send an enquiry, WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply to that conversation.
  • Hosting — the site is served through Cloudflare, which processes request logs as any web host does.

Your choices

You can ask us what enquiry details we hold, ask for them to be corrected, or ask us to delete them by writing to info@dreamwallspaints.com or calling +91 88550 87013.
