    Interior paint options

    Inside, where
    light does the work.

    Interior coatings are judged up close, in lamplight, by hand. Washability, low odour and a even film matter more than raw weather resistance.

    • Luxury interior emulsion Grand Glo — a smooth, high-opacity finish for living rooms and feature walls.
    • Washable interior emulsion Safire 100% Washable — scrubbable films for corridors, kitchens and kids' rooms.
    • Interior–exterior emulsion Iposh (Interior / Exterior) — one system for balconies, parapets and rooms that face the sun.
    • Primer + putty base Safire and Max Plus primers over acrylic wall putty for a sealed, level substrate.
    Choosing a coat
    Film
    Softer, more flexible acrylic film
    Sheen
    Matt to silk; low glare under lamps
    Cleaning
    Washable grades resist scrubbing
    Odour
    Water-based systems
    Best for
    Bedrooms, living areas, ceilings, offices
    Film
    Harder, UV-stabilised and fade resistant
    Sheen
    Satin to high gloss for rain shedding
    Weathering
    Engineered for monsoon, sun and dust
    Adhesion
    Bonds to masonry, concrete and plaster
    Best for
    Facades, parapets, compounds, boundary walls

    Rule of thumb: match the coat to the wall's exposure. A washable interior emulsion indoors, a UV-stable exterior emulsion outside — never the reverse.

    Waterproof coatings

    Water finds
    every way in.

    In coastal Maharashtra, Goa and the Deccan monsoon belt, waterproofing is not a premium extra — it is the first specification. Dreamwalls builds water-based dampproof and protective coats alongside its decorative range.

    Dampproof coats

    Water-based dampproof emulsions applied to damp-prone walls before the decorative system begins.

    Floor & surface protection

    Anti-slip acrylic floor coats for wet areas, balconies and utility zones where gloss alone would be a hazard.

    Sealed masonry

    Masonry and water-based primers for chalky, porous plaster before the topcoats go on.

    Monsoon-ready exteriors

    Exterior emulsions intended for the UV load and humidity swings of the Pune–Konkan rain season.

    Specify in order. Substrate repair → primer → putty → primer → two finish coats. Skipping the primer is the most common cause of peeling paint in Indian buildings.
